About

FLOVIA Consulting is a founder-led practice working where hydrology, hydraulic modelling, GIS and remote sensing, climate-risk analysis and water auditing overlap.

Founder-led hydrology and geospatial analysis using HEC-RAS, HEC-HMS, QGIS and Python. A fuller profile is being prepared.

Services

  • Flood Risk AssessmentsFlood hazard and risk studies for sites and assets: flood modelling, flood-depth maps and mitigation screening.
  • Climate Risk AssessmentPhysical climate-hazard screening, scenario analysis, asset-level risk and adaptation options.
  • Storm Water ModellingCatchments, design storms, runoff and drainage capacity, with ponding hotspots and concept-level mitigation.
  • Water Audits and Water NeutralityWater balance, metering and data review, efficiency and reuse opportunities, and a water-neutrality roadmap.
  • Geo-Spatial AnalyticsGIS and remote sensing, terrain analysis, spatial screening, automated mapping and dashboards.
